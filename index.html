Your health and safety come first.

Memansa therapies are intended to complement—not replace—care from a qualified medical doctor. We do not diagnose conditions, guarantee results, or advise stopping prescribed medication. For chest pain, severe bleeding, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, or loss of consciousness, seek emergency medical care immediately.
